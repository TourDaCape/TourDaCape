ENQUIRY CHECKLIST

What to include in your message

Use the checklist below to help us understand your request. You can send the details by email or WhatsApp.

  • Name and preferred contact method
  • Arrival and departure dates
  • Number of travellers
  • Service: tour, transfer, accommodation or itinerary
  • Pickup area or accommodation location
  • Preferred destinations and activities
  • Budget range or special requirements, if relevant
