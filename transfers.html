HELP US QUOTE ACCURATELY

Information to include in your enquiry

The more complete your details, the easier it is to assess the request and respond with relevant options.

  • Travel date and preferred pickup time
  • Exact pickup and drop-off points
  • Number of adults and children, if applicable
  • Number and approximate size of bags
  • Flight details for airport pickups, if available
  • Waiting time, return journey or extra stops required
  • Whether the transfer is linked to a tour or activity

TRANSFER FAQs

Questions travellers often ask

Can I request an airport pickup before I arrive in South Africa?

Yes, you can send an enquiry in advance. The booking is only confirmed once the route, timing, availability and price have been agreed.

Can transport be combined with a tour?

You can ask about combining transport with an Aquila safari, Hermanus outing, Atlantis Dunes activity or another planned experience. Each activity and transfer needs to be confirmed.

Can I request transport for a group?

Yes, send the group size, luggage details and route. Suitable vehicle availability and capacity must be checked; do not assume a particular vehicle is available until confirmed.

Are prices shown on the website?

Quotes depend on the route, date, waiting time, stops and transport requirements. Request a quote for your specific journey.

What if my flight time changes?

Contact Tour Da Cape using the details on the Contact page as soon as possible. Any change to a confirmed arrangement must be acknowledged and agreed.
