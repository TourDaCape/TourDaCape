FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Transfers: what travellers often ask

Can I enquire about airport transfers before booking accommodation?

Yes. Share your likely destination or area and travel dates. The exact pickup and drop-off details should be confirmed before the transfer is finalised.

Can I request transport to Aquila safari?

Yes, you can enquire about transport and indicate whether you are also interested in a half-day or full-day safari. Safari availability, package inclusions and transport pricing must be confirmed.

Can I arrange transport to Hermanus for whale watching?

You can enquire about a Hermanus trip and transport. Whale activity is seasonal and sightings cannot be guaranteed; any boat-based activity needs separate availability confirmation.

Can you arrange transport to Atlantis Dunes and quad biking?

You can request transport, quad-biking information or both. Activity availability, provider requirements and costs must be confirmed before booking.

How much does a transfer cost?

Prices depend on the route, date, passenger requirements and requested service. We do not publish an unverified rate; request a quote for your specific journey.

Is my transfer booked when I send an enquiry?

No. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Wait for written confirmation of availability, price, timing and arrangements.

Where do you need to go?

Send your route, travel date and group details. We will review the request and confirm the options available.
