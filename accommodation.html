PLAN THE WHOLE STAY

Accommodation, tours and transport should work together

Choosing a place to stay is only one part of a smooth trip. A central base may suit a city-focused visit, while a longer route may require overnight stops. Your accommodation location can also affect pickup timing and the amount of time available at each attraction.

  • Share your arrival and departure dates.
  • Tell us whether accommodation is already booked.
  • List must-see destinations and any mobility or family needs.
  • Ask how transport and tours could fit around your stay.

A SIMPLE PLANNING CHECKLIST

What to tell us when asking about accommodation

Travel dates

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Accommodation assistance explained

Does Tour Da Cape own or operate the accommodation?

This website does not claim that Tour Da Cape owns hotels or guesthouses. Enquire about the type of accommodation assistance currently available and the exact booking process.

Can you guarantee a room or rate?

No availability or price should be treated as confirmed until the accommodation provider and terms have been checked for your dates.

Can accommodation be planned with transfers and tours?

You can send these requirements together so the itinerary can be considered as a whole. Each service, cost and availability must be confirmed.

Which area is best for a first visit?

That depends on your budget, interests, mobility and preferred atmosphere. Share what matters most and we can discuss location considerations.

What if I have already booked my accommodation?

Tell us the address or area and the dates of your stay. That helps when discussing tour pickups and transport arrangements.

How do I start?

Send your dates, group size, preferred area or accommodation style, budget range and the services you may need.
