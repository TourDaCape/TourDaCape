START WITH LOCATION

Compare popular areas to stay in Cape Town

Distances and travel times vary with traffic and the exact property. Consider what you want to do most, rather than choosing an area by name alone.

City Bowl & CBD

Best for first-time visitors, museums, restaurants and city sightseeing.

Consider: Central base for exploring city attractions; urban feel and traffic/parking can vary by street.

May suit: City sightseeing, business trips and shorter stays.

V&A Waterfront

Best for harbour attractions, dining, shopping and an easy-to-navigate visitor hub.

Consider: Convenient for waterfront activities; accommodation can be at a higher price point and busy at peak times.

May suit: First-time visitors, couples and families who value nearby attractions.

Discuss this area →

Sea Point & Atlantic Seaboard

Best for oceanfront walks, restaurants and coastal scenery.

Consider: Coastal atmosphere with access to city and beaches; check the exact location and transport needs.

May suit: Couples, walkers and travellers who enjoy the coast.

Camps Bay

Best for beach scenery, sunset views and restaurants close to the beachfront.

Consider: Scenic coastal setting; travel to central attractions may require transport and prices can vary significantly.

May suit: Beach-focused stays and special-occasion trips.

Constantia & the Winelands

Best for greener surroundings, wine-country outings and a slower pace.

Consider: Good for planning vineyard visits and scenic drives; less central for some city attractions.

May suit: Wine lovers, longer stays and travellers wanting a quieter base.

CHOOSE THE TYPE OF STAY

Accommodation options to consider

Each type has different trade-offs. Check the exact property’s facilities, cancellation terms, location and total cost before booking.

Hotels

May suit visitors who value reception services, on-site facilities and a more structured stay. Compare location, breakfast, parking and room configuration.

Guesthouses & B&Bs

Can offer a smaller-scale stay and local character. Check check-in times, breakfast arrangements, parking, accessibility and how late arrivals are handled.

Apartments & serviced stays

May be practical for longer visits or guests who want living space. Confirm cleaning, kitchen facilities, security, check-in arrangements and any additional fees.

Private villas & holiday homes

Can work for families or groups seeking shared space. Confirm bedroom and bathroom arrangements, occupancy rules, location, deposits and house policies.

Winelands stays

Consider a night or two outside the city if vineyards, food experiences and countryside scenery are central to your plans. Check transport needs in advance.

Multi-stop itineraries

For longer trips, you may choose more than one base. Consider travel time, luggage transfers and whether moving accommodation adds value to your itinerary.

MATCH THE STAY TO THE TRAVELLER

What matters for your type of trip?

Couples & short breaks

Prioritise the experiences you want close by: restaurants and city attractions, coastal walks or wine-country outings. Check transport after evening plans.

Families

Confirm room configuration, child policies, kitchen or laundry facilities, safe access and travel time to the activities you plan to do.

Groups & friends

Compare total cost, sleeping arrangements, bathrooms, shared spaces and the practicalities of transporting everyone together.

Business & longer stays

Consider proximity to meeting locations, reliable connectivity, work space, parking, check-in flexibility and the cost of repeated transfers.

ACCOMMODATION FAQs

Before you choose a place to stay

Which area is best for a first visit to Cape Town?

It depends on your priorities. The City Bowl is useful for city sightseeing; the Waterfront for harbour attractions; the Atlantic Seaboard for coastal atmosphere; and Constantia or the Winelands for a greener, wine-focused stay. Compare the exact property and transport needs.

Can Tour Da Cape book accommodation for me?

Send your dates and requirements so we can clarify what accommodation assistance is possible. Do not assume availability, a confirmed reservation or special rates until details have been confirmed in writing.

Can accommodation be combined with tours and transfers?

You can ask about coordinating your stay with tours and transport. Each component, its price and availability must be confirmed separately before final plans are made.

When should I enquire?

As early as practical, especially for peak travel periods or specific room requirements. Availability changes, and an enquiry is not itself a reservation.

What information should I send?

Include dates, number of nights, guest and room details, preferred areas, budget range, required facilities and the tours or transfers you may need.
