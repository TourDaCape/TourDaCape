MAKE YOUR PLAN PRACTICAL

A few details make a big difference

Start with your dates

Arrival and departure times affect how much can reasonably fit into your first and last days.

Rank your must-sees

Choose the experiences that matter most, then add optional stops if time allows.

Group nearby places

Planning by area can reduce unnecessary backtracking and make the day feel less rushed.

Check what needs booking

Tours, activities, attraction tickets and accommodation can have separate schedules, terms and availability.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Planning a trip to Cape Town

How many days should I spend in Cape Town?

It depends on your interests and whether you want to include the peninsula, Winelands, safari or coastal trips. Share the experiences you want most and we can discuss a realistic itinerary.

Can I combine tours and transport?

You can enquire about a combined plan. Each tour, activity and transport service must be confirmed, including its price, timing and inclusions.

Can you help if I have not booked accommodation yet?

Yes, you can include accommodation assistance in your enquiry. Share your dates, group size and preferences.

Is whale watching available all year?

Whale activity varies by season and conditions. Ask about the dates you are considering; sightings cannot be guaranteed.

Can I include quad biking at Atlantis Dunes?

You can enquire about quad biking and/or transport. Provider requirements, activity timing, price and availability need to be checked.

Will you confirm the final itinerary and prices?

Before you commit, request written confirmation of the services, timing, pricing, availability and any exclusions or provider conditions.

Tell us what you want your Cape trip to feel like

Share your dates, group size, must-see places, interests and transport or accommodation needs. We can then discuss the options that may fit.
