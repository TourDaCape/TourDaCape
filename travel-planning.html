SAMPLE ITINERARIES

How you could spend your time

These examples are flexible planning frameworks, not fixed or priced packages. Activities and route order should be confirmed against your dates, interests and availability.

3 DAYS

A first taste of Cape Town

  1. Day 1: City highlights and the Waterfront.
  2. Day 2: Cape Peninsula and coastal scenery.
  3. Day 3: Choose the Winelands or an activity.

Planning note: Keep the first and last days flexible around arrival and departure.

5 DAYS

City, coast and a day beyond

  1. Day 1: Arrive and settle in.
  2. Day 2: Explore Cape Town.
  3. Day 3: Cape Peninsula.
  4. Day 4: Winelands or Aquila safari.
  5. Day 5: A preferred activity or relaxed coastal time.

Planning note: Choose the experiences that matter most instead of trying to fit everything in.

7+ DAYS

A slower regional stay

  1. Allow time for the city and peninsula.
  2. Add a dedicated Winelands day.
  3. Consider seasonal Hermanus whale watching.
  4. Ask about Aquila safari or Atlantis Dunes options.
  5. Leave room for weather, rest and unplanned discoveries.

Planning note: For longer trips, consider whether changing bases will save travel time.

What to tell us when you enquire

A few details help us discuss suitable tour and transport options. You do not need a finished itinerary.

  • Arrival and departure dates
  • Number of travellers and children’s ages, if relevant
  • Interests: scenery, culture, wine, wildlife, beaches or activities
  • Where you are staying or where pickup is needed
  • Tours or transfers you want to enquire about
  • Must-see places, mobility or accessibility needs
