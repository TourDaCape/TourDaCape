SAMPLE ITINERARIES

How you could spend your time

These examples are flexible planning frameworks, not fixed or priced packages. Activities and route order should be confirmed against your dates, interests and availability.

3 DAYS

A first taste of Cape Town

  1. Day 1 — City and waterfront: focus on city landmarks, neighbourhoods and a relaxed waterfront visit.
  2. Day 2 — Peninsula scenery: plan a coastal route with selected stops and enough time for the journey.
  3. Day 3 — Choose your interest: consider the Winelands, an activity or another city experience.

Planning note: Keep the itinerary flexible and avoid stacking too many distant destinations into one day.

5 DAYS

City, coast and a day beyond

  1. Day 1: arrive, transfer to your accommodation and keep the schedule light.
  2. Day 2: explore Cape Town city highlights.
  3. Day 3: take a Cape Peninsula day trip.
  4. Day 4: choose a Winelands outing or enquire about an Aquila safari.
  5. Day 5: add a preferred activity, shopping time or a relaxed coastal day before departure.

Planning note: Choose the safari or Winelands day based on your interests, not simply to fit everything in.

7+ DAYS

A slower regional stay

  1. Allow time for Cape Town, city culture and the peninsula.
  2. Include a dedicated Winelands day or overnight stay if it suits your plans.
  3. Consider a seasonal Hermanus outing or another selected experience.
  4. Discuss an Aquila safari and its transport requirements if wildlife is a priority.
  5. Leave room for weather, rest, shopping and unexpected discoveries.

Planning note: Longer trips may benefit from more than one accommodation base, depending on your route.

PLAN WITH REALISTIC TRAVEL TIME

Practical things to consider

Allow time between stops

Travel times change with traffic, road conditions, the route and how long you spend at each attraction. Avoid relying on a tightly packed schedule.

Check seasonality

Wildlife sightings, outdoor activities and ocean conditions vary. Confirm current schedules and conditions with the relevant operator before you travel.

Plan the logistics

Consider pickup locations, luggage, accommodation check-in, return transport and whether a day trip works with your other bookings.

MAKE THE PLAN YOURS

What to tell us when you enquire

You do not need a finished itinerary. A few details help start a useful conversation about suitable options.

  • Your arrival and departure dates
  • Number of travellers and ages of children, if relevant
  • Interests: scenery, culture, wine, wildlife, beaches or activities
  • Accommodation already booked or still to be arranged
  • Pickup area and transport requirements
  • Any must-see places or accessibility considerations
