ABOUT TOUR DA CAPE

A considered way to explore the Cape

Tour Da Cape helps visitors explore travel possibilities around Cape Town and the Western Cape through tour enquiries, transport planning and personalised trip discussions. We aim to make it easier to explain what you want to experience and understand what can be arranged for your dates.

WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Travel planning that starts with the person taking the trip

Every visitor has different priorities. Some want to see the major sights, some are travelling with family, and others want to connect a safari, coastal visit or Winelands outing with their stay. Our role is to understand the request and discuss suitable options without promising services before they have been confirmed.

Listen first

We encourage you to share your dates, group size, interests, budget range and any practical requirements so the enquiry is based on your actual trip.

Connect the details

Tours, transport and accommodation can affect one another. We help you raise those needs together so the overall plan can be considered.

Confirm clearly

Prices, availability, activity inclusions and supplier conditions should be confirmed before you commit. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.

HOW WE CAN HELP

From a single transfer to a wider itinerary

Depending on what is available for your dates, you can enquire about Cape Town sightseeing, Cape Peninsula trips, Winelands experiences, Aquila safari options, Hermanus whale watching, Atlantis Dunes quad biking and transport, accommodation assistance or multi-day planning.

Tell us what you already have booked and where you would like help. We will clarify what can be offered and what needs separate confirmation.

OUR ENQUIRY APPROACH

What you can expect from the process

We want visitors to understand the next step and the difference between asking about a service and confirming a booking.

You share your plans

Send dates, group size, preferred destinations and what help you need.

We clarify the request

We may need to confirm details such as pickup location, activity choice or accommodation preferences.

Options are checked

Price, supplier availability, terms and service inclusions need to be checked for the specific request.

You decide after confirmation

Review the details provided before deciding whether to proceed with a booking.

A FEW IMPORTANT NOTES

Clear information matters

Are all services available every day?

No. Availability depends on dates, providers, schedules, weather where relevant and the details of your request.

Are prices shown on the website final?

Do not treat general website information as a confirmed quote. Request a quote for your dates and requirements and check the written details before booking.

Can I request a tailor-made itinerary?

Yes, you can send a trip-planning enquiry with your dates, interests and preferred pace. Any proposed activities, transport and accommodation remain subject to confirmation.

How do I contact Tour Da Cape?

Use the Contact page to share your requirements. The contact details listed there are the appropriate starting point for an enquiry.
